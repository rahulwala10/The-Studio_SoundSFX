# The Studio Sound library: credits and licences

Every sound and track here is free to use in your videos, including monetised social media, with no credit needed.

- Sound effects: Freesound uploads under Creative Commons 0 (public domain dedication), Kenney audio packs (CC0), and a few sounds made for The Studio.

Each file was trimmed, faded, high-passed at 50 Hz and loudness-normalised (effects to -16 LUFS momentary max, ambiences to -20, music to -20 LUFS integrated).
