**The Studio Sound v1.0**: captions, sound effects and music for Reels, by Rahul Wala.

Open it: https://rahulwala10.github.io/The-Studio_SoundSFX/
How it works: https://rahulwala10.github.io/The-Studio_SoundSFX/how-it-works.html

**What's in it**
- Everything in The Studio Captions v1.2: 47 templates, automatic stand-out words, Hindi and Hinglish to English captions, Apply to all, your own templates, Best-quality and Instagram-size export.
- **Sounds tab.** Measures your voice, pauses, cuts and mood, then places sound effects on moments with a job (hook, new point, list items, twist, money, good news, photos and messages, call to action, cuts) and a music bed that matches the mood.
- **Subtle, Punchy or Off.** Effects are set against your measured voice and never go above it; music sits 15 to 18 LU under your voice and ducks while you talk.
- **Preview, audition, mute and swap** every sound; pick your own music; effects and music level sliders.
- **Save the sound track** as a .mov for Edits (Audio → Import) or a .wav for CapCut.
- **Library:** 119 CC0 effects and 32 public-domain music beds, credited in `sounds/CREDITS.md`.
- Keeps its own projects, separate from The Studio Captions.

**Downloads**
- `the-studio-sound-v1.0-windows.zip` and `the-studio-sound-v1.0-sounds.zip`: the laptop copy. Unzip both in the same place, then double-click `Start The Studio Sound.bat` (http://localhost:5179).
- `source/the-studio-sound-source.zip` (repository root): the full source code.
